SEC Rule 10b5-1: Trading Plans

Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This rule addresses insider trading liability and provides affirmative defenses for trading plans. It applies to all persons subject to Section 10(b) and Rule 10b-1 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.

Section 2: Definitions
(a) "Trading plan" means a written plan for trading securities that meets the requirements of this rule.
(b) "Insider" means any person who possesses material non-public information.
(c) "Material information" means information that a reasonable investor would consider important in making an investment decision.

Section 3: Affirmative Defense Requirements
A person shall not be liable for insider trading if the trade was made pursuant to a binding contract, instruction, or trading plan that:
(a) Was entered into before the person became aware of material non-public information
(b) Specifies the amount, price, and date of the securities to be traded
(c) Includes a formula or algorithm for determining such amounts, prices, and dates

Section 4: Good Faith Requirement
The affirmative defense is available only if the contract, instruction, or plan was entered into in good faith and not as part of a plan to evade the prohibitions of Section 10(b). A person may not modify or deviate from the plan while in possession of material non-public information.

Section 5: Cooling-Off Period
For directors and officers, the first trade under the plan may not occur until the later of:
(a) 90 days after the adoption of the plan
(b) Two business days after the filing of certain periodic reports

Section 6: Certifications
Each person adopting a trading plan must certify that they are not aware of material non-public information about the issuer.

Section 7: Recordkeeping Requirements
Issuers must maintain records of all trading plans for at least five years and make them available to the Commission upon request.

Section 8: Effective Date
This rule shall become effective 30 days after publication in the Federal Register.

Section 9: Severability
If any provision of this rule is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.
